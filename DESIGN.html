html-revisor: design

Everything html-revisor adds to a page, drawn with the real stylesheet. This page links client.css; change the stylesheet and both the tool and this page change.

Palette

Custom properties on :root. Dark mode redefines the neutrals and keeps the accent. Values are read from the stylesheet when the page loads.

accent: hover outline, stripe, marker, Save, focus
accent-strong: hover on marker and Save
error: failed or stale save
surface: the directive box
field: textareas
button: Delete
line: borders
text
muted: labels, hint
toast

On the page

The tool puts two classes on page elements. Everything else lives in its own layer above the page.

Hovering an element outlines it, and the cursor becomes a copy cursor: click to leave a directive or to edit the text.

.hrv-hover: 2px dashed accent outline.

This paragraph has a directive. It gets a stripe on its left edge, and a marker at its top-left corner.

✎

.hrv-has-directive: 3px inset accent stripe. .hrv-marker: 18px, 9px up and left of the element; its tooltip is the directive.

Directive box

Opens 20px below the element's top, 320px wide or the viewport less 16px. Saves 0.4 s after typing stops; Save and ⌘↵ close it.

Toast

Bottom right of the window. Fades in; leaves after 1.5 s, or 4 s for an error. The stale-file toast stays.

Type

Everything in the layer: --hrv-font, 13px system-ui at line height 1.4. Labels and the hint: --hrv-small, 11px, muted.
