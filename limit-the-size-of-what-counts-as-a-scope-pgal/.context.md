On the Mintlify test page, wrapper divs are scopes whose markdown runs to 288 KB; rendering it takes up to 1.4 s, and nobody edits that in a textarea. Decide a rule: a cap on text length or element count, or on depth of nested blocks, or wrappers with a single block child aren't scopes. Rules live in scopes.mjs; the decision goes in the SCOPES-DESIGN.html Decided table with a case that shows it. A too-big element can still take a directive; it just has no Content field.
