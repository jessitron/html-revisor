Guesses

Guess: the clock is performance.now() plus an anchor that starts at performance.timeOrigin and moves to the wall clock when the two part by more than 2 ms (makeClock in interface/telemetry.mjs), not a bare performance.timeOrigin + performance.now().

Why: on macOS the monotonic clock stops while the laptop sleeps, and the server runs for days; a bare sum would stamp spans hours in the past next to browser spans on the wall clock.

If wrong: drop the anchor check in makeClock and its sleep case in test/telemetry.test.mjs.

Guess: pass a time to every start, end, and exception through the public API, not set span.startTime in a span processor's onStart.

Why: the processor route needs the SDK's private _performanceStartTime to stay exact. The cost: any new span.end() or addEvent on a server span without a time falls back to whole milliseconds. Only inSpan, requestSpan, and one recordException in server.mjs do so today.

If wrong: replace the spanTime() arguments in interface/telemetry.mjs and server.mjs with a span processor in startTelemetry.
